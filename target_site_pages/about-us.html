About Alven Homz

Your home, our continued commitment

The trusted home services vertical of Abhee Ventures, built on 17 years of legacy.

Who we are

Beyond the keys to your home

At Abhee Ventures, our relationship with homeowners has always gone beyond handing over the keys. Over 17 years of building quality homes, we have earned the trust of our homeowners, and Alven Homz is our next step in strengthening that bond.

Alven Homz is the dedicated in-house service vertical of Abhee Ventures. We were created to support you long after you take possession of your home. Built on Abhee's values of trust, care and quality, we offer reliable and cost-effective solutions that make homeownership easier and more convenient.

From interior design and home transformations to rental, resale and property management, we bring essential home services together under one roof, with one team you can rely on.

What drives us

Mission, vision and values

Our Mission

  • To make homeownership simple, convenient and worry-free through reliable, transparent and cost-effective home services.

Our Vision

  • To be the most trusted home services partner for homeowners, carrying forward the legacy of quality and trust that defines Abhee Ventures.

Our Values

Care · Trust · Value

  • Care in how we look after your home and your needs.
  • Trust in every commitment we make and keep.
  • Value in every solution we deliver.
