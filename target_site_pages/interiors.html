How we work

Our Design Process

A clear, six-step journey from first conversation to final handover.

  1. 01

    Free Consultation

    Meet our designer at your site or our studio to discuss your space, style and timelines.

  2. 02

    Requirement Discussion

    We understand how your family lives, your storage needs and your budget.

  3. 03

    Design Planning

    Layouts, mood boards and 3D views so you can see your home before work begins.

  4. 04

    Material Selection

    Choose finishes, fabrics and hardware from curated, quality-checked options.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Dedicated project manager, factory-finished modules and regular site updates.

  6. 06

    Final Handover

    Deep cleaning, styling and a detailed walkthrough of your finished home.
