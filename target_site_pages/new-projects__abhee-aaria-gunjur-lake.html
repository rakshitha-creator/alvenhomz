Project Overview

A lake-facing community opposite Gunjur Lake, with 1,017 homes across five towers on 12 acres. Nearly 85% of the land is open space, including a 2-acre forest zone, and two clubhouses of about 60,000 sq.ft offer 60+ amenities — from an infinity pool and mini theatre to a business centre and work-from-home stations.
