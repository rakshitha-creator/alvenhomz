Project Overview

A low-rise community of 462 homes in three B+G+4 blocks on 6.75 acres, directly opposite Wipro SEZ off Sarjapur Road. Every home is planned with no common walls, and amenities include a pool, indoor and outdoor gyms, a skating rink and an outdoor workstation and library. Nearing completion, with possession targeted for August 2026. BDA and RERA approved.
